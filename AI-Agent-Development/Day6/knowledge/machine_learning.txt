Machine Learning (ML) is a branch of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that enables computers to learn from data and improve their performance without being explicitly programmed for every task. It uses algorithms and statistical techniques to identify patterns, analyze information, and make predictions or decisions. Machine Learning is widely used in various fields, such as healthcare, education, banking, business, robotics, and online shopping. Some common applications of Machine Learning include speech recognition, image recognition, recommendation systems, fraud detection, and self-driving cars. The three main types of Machine Learning are Supervised Learning, Unsupervised Learning, and Reinforcement Learning. It helps computers solve complex problems, automate tasks, and make smarter decisions. Due to its growing importance, Machine Learning has become one of the most popular and powerful technologies in the modern world.
