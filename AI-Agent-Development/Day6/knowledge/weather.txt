Weather refers to the condition of the atmosphere at a particular place and time. It includes different elements such as temperature, humidity, rainfall, wind speed, air pressure, and cloud cover. Weather can change from day to day or even within a few hours. The main types of weather include sunny, rainy, cloudy, windy, snowy, and stormy. Weather plays an important role in our daily lives because it affects agriculture, transportation, clothing, and outdoor activities. Weather forecasting helps people predict future weather conditions and prepare for natural events such as heavy rainfall, storms, and heatwaves.
